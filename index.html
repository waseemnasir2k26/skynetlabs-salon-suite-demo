Tenant voices

Built by pros, for pros.

Questions

Before you tour.

What's actually included in the rent?+

Water, electric, Wi-Fi, towel service, laundry of capes & towels, common-area cleaning, parking, and a $25/wk backbar credit. The number on the page is the number you pay.

Lease term and deposit?+

90-day initial commitment, then month-to-month. $250 deposit holds the suite for 14 days; refundable in full if you don't sign, applied to your first week if you do.

Can I customize the suite?+

Paint, signage, lighting swaps, and IG-ready photo walls — yes. Plumbing changes — by request. Anything you add and want to take with you stays yours.

How is after-hours access handled?+

Keyless entry tied to your phone, 24/7. Building has 12 cameras + monitored alarm. Late-night clients are welcome — we'll add their name to the visitor list with one tap.

Do I need my own insurance?+

Yes — a basic beauty-pro general liability policy ($1M / $2M) is required. We'll send a sample COI and a partner quote ($18/mo) if you don't have one yet.

What if it's not the right fit?+

Tour, walk through, ask anything. If it's not your space, full deposit refund within 3 business days. No tour-trap, no hard sell.
