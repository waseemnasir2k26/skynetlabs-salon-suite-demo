# Salon Suite Rental — Research Pack

## 1. Competitor Sameness Gap

12 chains audited (Sola, Phenix, MY SALON, Salons by JC, IMAGE Studios, Salon Lofts, Salon Plaza, Studio Salon Suites, Indie, Ovation, plus Salon Renter & MeetSpa marketplaces).

**100% of chain landlords:**
- Hide pricing — only marketplaces show rates
- Use vague-aspirational headlines ("dreams," "extraordinary," "thrive")
- Run corporate blue/white palettes + stocky lifestyle photos
- Recycle the same 4 differentiators (24/7, month-to-month, utilities, "community") — table stakes now
- Force tour-first lead funnels with no online reserve / deposit option

**The opening:** boutique editorial aesthetic + transparent all-in pricing + online refundable deposit. The buyer (the beauty pro) already curates their IG that way. Site that matches their visual brand short-circuits the tour-first funnel.

## 2. Top 3 Hooks (lead with these)

1. "Your suite, your brand, your IG-ready set." — editorial interiors, photographed for content not franchise brochure
2. Transparent all-in pricing + reserve online — skip the tour gauntlet, refundable deposit
3. Built for the 6-figure solo pro — concierge onboarding, curated tenant roster

## 5. Schema Pick

`HealthAndBeautyBusiness` (subtype of LocalBusiness) — broadest correct parent for multi-discipline tenant mix. Shipping with `amenityFeature`, `Offer` per tier with `UnitPriceSpecification`, and `FAQPage` for 6 buyer objections. Skip `BeautySalon` (implies you do the service) and `RealEstateAgent` (consumer search won't trigger it).

## 6. EEAT Checklist (10)

1. State cosmetology license # + city occupational permit visible in footer
2. "X locations / Y suites / Z tenants since [year]" stat block above fold
3. Founder/operator bio with industry years + LinkedIn
4. 8-12 named tenant testimonials w/ IG handle + headshot + service niche
5. 60-90s walkthrough video (suite + common areas + parking lot at night)
6. Security: 24/7 keyless brand named, camera count, alarm-monitoring vendor
7. Insurance: tenant GL requirement + sample COI + partner quote
8. Utilities clarity table — included vs add-on
9. Lease terms in plain English: month-to-month vs 12mo, deposit, early-exit
10. Real photos (not stock) with EXIF/geo + recent Google reviews embed (4.6+)

## 7. Page Must-Answer Questions (per beauty-pro buyer survey)

- What's included in rent? (water, power, wifi, laundry, towels, backbar, reception, parking)
- Lease term + deposit + cancellation? (week-to-week vs 12-mo, refundable?)
- Suite size + what fits (chair count, shampoo bowl, plumbing for nails/lash bed)
- 24/7 access + security + after-hours client policy
- How tours work + can I reserve before touring
- Who's already here (tenant mix — avoid same-service overlap)
- Marketing/booking support (directory listing, GlossGenius/Vagaro, social features)
- Insurance, license, business setup help
- Move-in/customization rules (paint, signage, branding)
- Contract: what happens if I leave early
